About Butterflies

Small creatures with extraordinary beauty.

Butterflies are fascinating insects known for their colorful wings and graceful flight. They belong to the order Lepidoptera and play an important role in nature by helping pollinate flowers. Their amazing life cycle reminds us that change can lead to something beautiful.

Beautiful Butterfly Species

Meet some of the world's most remarkable butterflies.

Monarch Butterfly

Famous for its orange-and-black wings, the monarch is known for its remarkable long-distance migration.

Blue Morpho

This tropical butterfly is celebrated for its brilliant, shimmering blue wings.

Swallowtail

Many swallowtails have striking patterns and distinctive tail-like extensions on their hindwings.

The Butterfly Life Cycle

Four amazing stages of transformation.

1. Egg

A butterfly lays tiny eggs on a suitable host plant.

2. Caterpillar

The larva eats and grows, shedding its skin as needed.

3. Chrysalis

Inside the chrysalis, the insect transforms its body.

4. Adult

The adult butterfly emerges, expands its wings, and flies.

Did You Know?

Fun facts about these incredible insects.

  • Butterflies taste using sensors on their feet.
  • Butterfly wings are covered in tiny scales.
  • Butterflies use their long proboscis to drink nectar.
  • Many butterflies help pollinate flowering plants.
  • Butterflies are cold-blooded and depend on external warmth.

Protect Our Butterflies

Butterflies need healthy habitats to survive. Plant native flowers, avoid unnecessary pesticides, protect natural spaces, and provide suitable host plants for caterpillars. Every small action can help nature thrive.
